OPERATIONS BRIEF

Field manual

Defend both tissue nodes and keep your suit intact. If either node fails, the mission ends. Follow the node markers and hold repair near damaged tissue.

Face clear ground to deploy your auto turret. It briefly slows enemies it hits. Place it near one approach while you defend the other.

Viruses recoil before lunging. Spores swell before spitting a slow projectile. Move sideways to dodge either attack. Shoot latched bacilli before they tear more tissue.

Armored pathogens block shots from the front. Flank them to hit their exposed tissue. Splitting pathogens release two smaller viruses when killed.

In Capillary Junction, the attack countdown shows which node is next and what is coming. Between waves, choose an inlet reinforcement, ammo, or turret redeployment. To move an active turret, select Redeploy turret, face clear ground, and press Q or tap Turret before the break ends.

Open gashes drain tissue even after the attacker is gone. Follow the gash marker and hold repair in range to seal it. Once Capillary Junction is secure, extract or stay for an optional bonus wave.

At Heart Valve, shoot the colony through the opening and repair the valve wound while it is closed. You can repair the other node at any time. Restore both to secure the chamber.

Watch the organ status during later missions for hazard warnings and advice. Your campaign checkpoint keeps completed missions and your field upgrade. The final report shows tissue integrity across all eight missions.
